Confidentiality Policy

Last updated: 18 May 2026

1. Preamble

Confidentiality is the cornerstone of KEY Investigations' operations. As an insurance investigation agency, we routinely handle highly sensitive information including personal data, financial records, medical histories, surveillance materials, and proprietary client data. The integrity of our work and the trust of our clients depend on our unwavering commitment to information security.

This Confidentiality Policy establishes the framework by which all employees, contractors, field agents, and associates of KEY Investigations are bound to protect confidential information throughout its lifecycle -- from collection through processing, storage, dissemination, and eventual destruction.

2. Non-Disclosure Agreement Framework

2.1 Employee and Contractor NDAs

Every individual who joins KEY Investigations -- whether as a full-time employee, part-time contractor, field agent, or intern -- is required to execute a comprehensive Non-Disclosure Agreement before accessing any confidential information. The NDA covers:

  • A broad definition of confidential information encompassing investigation data, client identities, methodologies, report templates, and internal systems
  • Obligations to protect confidential information during and after the term of engagement, with a survival period of 5 years post-termination
  • Restrictions on copying, transmitting, or discussing confidential information outside authorised channels
  • Acknowledgement that breach of confidentiality may result in immediate termination, legal action, and recovery of damages

2.2 Client NDAs

We execute mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements with all clients to establish reciprocal confidentiality obligations. These agreements define:

  • The scope of confidential information exchanged between parties
  • Permitted use of investigation findings (limited to the stated purpose of the engagement)
  • Restrictions on sharing investigation reports with unauthorised third parties
  • Duration of confidentiality obligations and conditions for disclosure

2.3 Vendor and Third-Party NDAs

Any third-party service provider (IT vendors, cloud infrastructure providers, legal consultants, forensic laboratories) who may have access to confidential information must execute an NDA and demonstrate compliance with our security standards before engagement.

3. Data Handling Procedures

3.1 Classification

All information handled by KEY Investigations is classified into four tiers:

Level 1 -- Highly Restricted

Active investigation evidence, surveillance footage, witness statements, claimant medical records, fraud intelligence. Access limited to assigned investigator and case supervisor only.

Level 2 -- Restricted

Completed investigation reports, client referral data, case notes, internal review comments. Access limited to authorised team members and QC reviewers.

Level 3 -- Internal

Company policies, training materials, standard operating procedures, internal communications. Accessible to all KEY Investigations employees.

Level 4 -- Public

Marketing materials, published policies, website content, public-facing company information. No access restrictions.

3.2 Need-to-Know Principle

Access to confidential information is granted strictly on a need-to-know basis. An investigator assigned to a motor claim case shall not have access to unrelated health claim files. Case assignments and data access are controlled through our portal's role-based access control system.

3.3 Transmission Protocols

  • Investigation reports and evidence are transmitted exclusively through the secure client portal or encrypted email
  • Personal messaging applications (WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.) shall not be used for transmitting investigation data, evidence photographs, or client information
  • Physical documents containing confidential information must be transported in sealed, tamper-evident envelopes
  • Verbal discussions of case details must not occur in public places or in the presence of unauthorised persons

4. Employee Confidentiality Obligations

All personnel of KEY Investigations are bound by the following obligations:

  • Never discuss investigation details, client identities, or case outcomes with family, friends, or any unauthorised person
  • Never post or share investigation-related content on social media, personal blogs, or public forums
  • Lock workstations and secure devices when unattended; use strong, unique passwords for all systems
  • Report any suspected confidentiality breach or data leak immediately to the designated Compliance Officer
  • Return all confidential materials, devices, and access credentials upon termination of employment or contract
  • Not retain personal copies of investigation files, photographs, reports, or any confidential data after departure
  • Cooperate fully with any internal investigation into suspected breaches

Violation of these obligations may result in disciplinary action up to and including immediate termination, legal proceedings, and claims for damages.

5. Evidence Security

Given the sensitive nature of investigation evidence, the following security measures are enforced:

5.1 Physical Evidence

  • Physical evidence is stored in locked, access-controlled storage facilities with CCTV surveillance
  • Chain of custody is documented for every item from collection to storage or submission
  • Only authorised personnel may access evidence storage areas, with entry and exit logged
  • Evidence handling requires gloves and tamper-evident packaging where applicable

5.2 Digital Evidence

  • All digital evidence (photographs, videos, documents) is stored on encrypted servers with access audit trails
  • Cryptographic hash values (SHA-256) are computed for all digital evidence files at the time of capture to ensure integrity
  • Original evidence files are preserved in read-only format; working copies are created for analysis
  • Removable media (USB drives, SD cards) used for evidence transfer must be company-issued, encrypted, and tracked
  • Detailed evidence handling procedures are available in our Evidence Handling Policy

6. Digital Security Measures

KEY Investigations maintains comprehensive digital security controls:

  • Encryption: AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit for all systems handling confidential data
  • Access controls: Role-based access with row-level security in the client portal database, enforced via Supabase RLS policies
  • Authentication: Multi-factor authentication required for all administrative and portal accounts
  • Monitoring: Continuous monitoring of system access logs, with automated alerts for anomalous activity
  • Device management: Company-issued devices are encrypted, remotely wipeable, and subject to mobile device management policies
  • Network security: VPN required for remote access to internal systems; firewall and intrusion detection systems in place
  • Backup: Automated encrypted backups with geographically distributed storage and regular restoration testing
  • Vulnerability management: Regular security assessments, penetration testing, and timely patching of all systems

7. Breach Notification Process

In the event of a confirmed or suspected confidentiality breach, the following process is activated:

1

Immediate Containment (0-2 hours)

The breach is reported to the Compliance Officer. Affected systems or access points are immediately isolated. Compromised credentials are revoked. Preliminary assessment of scope and impact is conducted.

2

Investigation (2-24 hours)

Forensic analysis of the breach is initiated. The root cause, extent of data exposure, and affected parties are identified. All actions are documented with timestamps.

3

Notification (24-72 hours)

Affected clients are notified with details of the breach, data involved, and remedial actions taken. Regulatory authorities (Data Protection Board of India) are notified as required by applicable law.

4

Remediation (Ongoing)

Security vulnerabilities are patched. Affected processes are reviewed and strengthened. Additional training is provided to personnel. A post-incident report is prepared with lessons learned and preventive measures.

8. Confidentiality Training

All KEY Investigations personnel receive mandatory confidentiality and data protection training upon onboarding and at annual intervals thereafter. Training covers data classification, handling procedures, digital security best practices, social engineering awareness, and the consequences of confidentiality breaches. Completion is tracked, and personnel who fail to complete training within the specified timeframe are restricted from accessing confidential systems until training is completed.
