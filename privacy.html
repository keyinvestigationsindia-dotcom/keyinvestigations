Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 May 2026

1. Introduction

KEY Investigations ("we," "our," or "the Company") is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of all personal data entrusted to us. As an insurance investigation agency operating across India, we handle sensitive information in the course of our professional services, and we recognise the critical importance of safeguarding such data.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect personal information obtained through our website (keyinvestigations.in), our client portal, and our investigation services. This policy is compliant with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 as applicable.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information through various channels depending on the nature of our engagement:

2.1 Information Provided Directly

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone number, business address
  • Client onboarding data: company registration details, authorised signatory information, GST identification numbers
  • Claim referral details: policy numbers, claim numbers, insured party information, incident descriptions
  • Communication records: emails, portal messages, call logs related to active investigations

2.2 Information Collected During Investigations

  • Claimant and witness statements, identification documents, and contact information
  • Photographic and video evidence of incident sites, vehicles, property, and related subjects
  • Medical and hospital records (obtained with proper authorisation)
  • Vehicle registration, repair, and service records
  • Geographic and location data related to investigation sites
  • Public records, court filings, and government database information

2.3 Technical Information

  • Browser type, IP address, device type, and operating system when visiting our website
  • Login timestamps, session data, and portal usage analytics
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies as described in Section 6

3. How We Use Your Information

We process personal data strictly for legitimate business purposes:

  • Conducting insurance claim investigations as contracted by our clients (insurers, TPAs, legal firms)
  • Preparing investigation reports, evidence dossiers, and court-admissible documentation
  • Communicating with clients regarding case status, findings, and deliverables
  • Managing client accounts, invoicing, and payment processing
  • Complying with legal obligations, regulatory requirements, and court orders
  • Improving our services, training investigators, and enhancing quality control processes
  • Maintaining the security and functionality of our website and client portal

4. Data Storage and Security

All personal data is stored on secure, encrypted servers. Our client portal is hosted on Supabase infrastructure with enterprise-grade security, including:

  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit
  • Row-level security (RLS) policies ensuring data isolation between clients
  • Role-based access controls limiting data visibility to authorised personnel only
  • Regular security audits, vulnerability assessments, and penetration testing
  • Automated backups with geographically distributed redundancy

Physical evidence and printed documents are stored in locked, access-controlled facilities. Digital evidence is maintained with tamper-proof checksums and audit trails to ensure integrity.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected:

  • Active investigation files: retained for the duration of the investigation plus any litigation period
  • Completed case records: retained for a minimum of 8 years from case closure, or longer if required by law or ongoing litigation
  • Client account data: retained for the duration of the business relationship plus 5 years
  • Website analytics: anonymised and retained for up to 2 years
  • Upon expiry of retention periods, data is securely destroyed using industry-standard methods

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience:

  • Essential cookies: Required for website functionality, authentication, and security. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand website traffic and user behaviour to improve our services. These are anonymised.
  • Session cookies: Maintain your login state in the client portal. These expire when you close your browser or after a defined timeout period.

We do not use advertising cookies or share browsing data with advertising networks. You may manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

7. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. We may share information with the following parties only as necessary:

  • Our clients: Insurance companies, TPAs, and legal firms who have engaged us for investigation services, limited to information relevant to the specific investigation
  • Legal authorities: Courts, tribunals, and law enforcement agencies when required by law or court order
  • Service providers: Cloud hosting, secure communication, and IT infrastructure providers who are bound by strict data processing agreements
  • Professional advisors: Auditors, legal counsel, and quality assurance reviewers, subject to confidentiality obligations

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law and the legitimate requirements of active investigations, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: Request confirmation of whether we hold your personal data and obtain a copy of such data
  • Right to correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your personal data where it is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected, subject to legal retention obligations
  • Right to withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing
  • Right to grievance redressal: Lodge a complaint with us or with the relevant Data Protection Board of India

Please note that certain rights may be limited where disclosure would compromise an active investigation, obstruct the prevention or detection of fraud, or conflict with legal or regulatory obligations.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we become aware that personal data of a minor has been collected without appropriate parental consent, we will take steps to delete such data promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. Any material changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
